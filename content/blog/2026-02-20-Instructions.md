## Filming Guidelines

- Position the camera at an appropriate distance to capture the entire fencing strip within the frame

- Ensure the scoring lights remain clearly visible at all times during recording  
  (Current score-box tracking cannot handle partially obscured or off-screen lights.)

- When placing light boxes during analysis, keep **only the score LED** inside each box — avoid other red or green in the frame (lamé, signs, clothing, glare) inside the selection

- Minimize camera movement to maintain a stable and consistent view


## Analysis Procedure

![Fencing filming example showing fencers and scoring lights](/static/blog/fencing-demo.png)

1. Enter Email (analysis result will be sent to you)
2. Select Fencer 1
3. Select Fencer 2
4. Drag the Fencer 1 and Fencer 2 light boxes onto each fencer's scoring light (either side of the strip); enlarge if needed. Each box should cover only that LED — no other red or green nearby
6. Run the analysis

The analysis is GPU-intensive and may take 2 to 15 minutes to complete, depending on the length of your 
video and the number of pending jobs. You are not required to wait for completion — the results will be 
sent to your email address.
