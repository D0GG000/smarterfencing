## About the Project

My name is **Jordan Fan**, and I’m a junior in high school. I’ve been fencing for several years, and while I’m currently an **E-rated fencer**, I’ve never quite had that “breakthrough” moment. I ke
pt asking myself whether there was a better, more **data-driven** way to analyze and improve my fencing.

As I trained and competed, a few questions kept coming up:

- Where was I landing most of my touches?
- Which techniques was I most — or least — successful with?
- Was I being too defensive early in a match?

I could, of course, watch videos of my own bouts, but that process was **time-intensive and laborious**. It always felt inefficient, and I was convinced there had to be a better way to extract mean
ingful insights from fencing footage.

---

## Why SmarterFencing.ai

That’s what led me to start **SmarterFencing.ai**.

Initially, the goal was simple: build a tool that could help me analyze my own fencing and identify patterns I couldn’t easily see on my own. But as I researched further and dove deeper into the te
chnology, I realized something bigger was possible.

I could create an **AI system** that helps **fencers and coaches** better understand performance by revealing patterns that are difficult to detect in real time.

---

## A Personal Motivation

On a personal level, fencing has been a huge part of my life. More recently, this project has become just as important. While I may not become a **world-class épéeist**, I’ve found meaning in figur
ing out how I can give back to the sport that has given me so much.

Fencing is a **niche sport**, and unlike more popular sports such as boxing or basketball, this kind of **automated, data-driven analysis** doesn’t really exist yet. My hope is to help make trainin
g more insightful and effective by giving athletes clearer, more actionable feedback.

---

## What’s Next

Soon, other fencers will be able to upload their own fencing videos. As long as they follow my recommended filming guidelines, the AI model I’ve built should be able to provide **valuable insights*
* for both athletes and coaches.
