About

Why this exists

My name is Jordan Fan and I'm a junior in high school. I've been fencing for a few years. While I'm an E-rated fencer, I've never quite had a "breakthrough" moment. I've always wondered if there was a better way to dissect and improve my own fencing using a data-driven approach.

Where was I landing most of my touches? Which technique was I the most (or least) successful at? Was I being too defensive early in the match?

Watching my own videos helped, but it was slow. SmarterFencing.AI started as a way to pull the patterns that are hard to see in real time—and to give that same clarity back to other fencers and coaches.

Fencing is niche; automatic bout data like this barely exists outside bigger sports. Upload a bout that follows the filming guidelines, and the analyzer should give you something you can actually train from.

Ready when you are—same account keeps your analyses private and revisitable.
