    What do these angles mean? ▼

    All angles are measured in 3D from the pose skeleton. Handedness (Auto / Left / Right) picks which arm is treated as the weapon arm.

    Arm Extension (% of 180)
    Elbow angle on the weapon arm (shoulder → elbow → wrist), shown as a percentage of a fully straight arm. 100% ≈ fully extended; lower values mean a more bent arm at the touch.
    Right Knee / Left Knee
    Knee angle (hip → knee → ankle). ~180° is straight; lower values mean a bent knee, as in a lunge.
    Dom. Shoulder
    Shoulder angle on the weapon side (torso → shoulder → elbow). Shows how far the weapon arm is raised or extended away from the body.
