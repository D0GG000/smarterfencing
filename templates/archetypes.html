Fencing archetypes

After an analysis, we classify each fencer’s scoring pattern into a style archetype. Here’s what each one means — and an elite fencer who shares a similar approach.

The Duelist The Sniper The Bolt The Tactician The Hybrid

⚔️ The Duelist

Style description

You like to take control of the bout. Rather than waiting for opportunities, you create them. Your fencing is built around initiative, commitment, and forcing your opponent to react. When you find the right distance and timing, your attacks become your greatest weapon.

Similar elite fencer

Yannick Borel 🇫🇷

A powerful attacker known for explosive direct actions, strong initiative, and the ability to impose his rhythm on opponents.

🎯 The Sniper

Style description

You are a patient hunter. Instead of forcing exchanges, you wait for the smallest mistake and punish it with precision. Your strength comes from timing, distance, and knowing exactly when to strike.

Similar elite fencer

Max Heinzer 🇨🇭

Known for exceptional timing, counterattacking ability, and the ability to turn small openings into decisive touches.

⚡ The Bolt

Style description

Speed is your advantage. You rely on sudden acceleration and explosive attacks to catch opponents before they can react. Your fencing creates moments where opponents feel like they have no time to adjust.

Similar elite fencer

Mohamed Elsayed 🇪🇬

An athletic and dynamic fencer known for fast attacks, aggressive movement, and explosive scoring actions.

♟️ The Tactician

Style description

You solve the puzzle of the bout. Instead of relying on one favorite weapon, you adjust your approach based on what your opponent gives you. Your greatest strength is versatility and unpredictability.

Similar elite fencer

Gauthier Grumier 🇫🇷

Known for his tactical intelligence, adaptability, and ability to control bouts through careful decision-making.

⚖️ The Hybrid

Style description

You cannot be defined by one approach. You blend different styles together, switching between attack, defense, and timing-based actions depending on what the bout demands. Your unpredictability is your advantage.

Similar elite fencer

Koki Kano 🇯🇵

A complete fencer who combines technical precision, tactical awareness, and multiple scoring solutions.
